Bonus meat — by branch & day

When a day's actual meat obtained comes in above the expected yield, the extra is bonus meat — good dresser/cutter performance. This is informational only: it does not change closing stock or what carries forward as tomorrow's opening meat.

Meat shortfall — by branch & day

When more meat is recorded sold (counter + hotel) or damaged on a day than was actually obtained from dressing that day, the gap is a shortfall — a data-entry slip or a genuine oversell. This is informational only: closing meat is already floored at zero regardless, so the shortfall never goes on to compound into tomorrow's opening meat.

Date Branch Category Actual meat Sold + damaged Shortfall Shortfall value

Live bird weight shortage — by branch & day

Birds and weight are tracked separately through the day. When every bird handled comes out fully accounted for (closing birds = 0) but the weight side still leaves something over, that weight has no bird left to carry it — it is recorded here as a shortage instead of being carried forward. Closing weight for that day is set to zero and tomorrow opens at zero, regardless of whether this report is ever opened.

Date Branch Category Closing birds Weight shortage Shortage value
